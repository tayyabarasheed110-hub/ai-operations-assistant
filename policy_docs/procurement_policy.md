# Procurement Policy

## Purpose
This policy governs how purchase orders are raised, approved, and fulfilled, and defines supplier requirements.

## Purchase Order Approval Limits
- Purchase orders **up to $1,000** may be approved by any employee holding order-creation authorization, with no additional sign-off required.
- Purchase orders **between $1,000 and $10,000** require approval from a department head in addition to the requester.
- Purchase orders **exceeding $10,000** require **two separate approvals**: a department head and a member of Finance, obtained independently before the order is placed.
- No single individual may approve their own purchase order, regardless of amount.

## Supplier Requirements
- Purchases must be made from suppliers on the approved supplier list wherever one exists for the relevant product category.
- Use of a supplier not on the approved list requires written justification and Finance sign-off, regardless of order value.
- Suppliers must be re-verified (tax status, business registration) at least **once every 12 months**.

## Quantity and Reorder Rules
- Standard reorder quantity for consumables (paper, office supplies) should not exceed a **90-day supply** based on historical usage, to avoid overstocking.
- Any single order for a quantity representing more than **6 months** of historical average usage requires a written justification note attached to the order.

## Emergency Procurement
- Emergency purchases (defined as required within **48 hours** to avoid an operational stoppage) may bypass the standard multi-day approval timeline but still require retroactive approval from a department head within **5 business days** of the purchase.
- Emergency procurement is capped at **$5,000** per incident; anything above this amount must follow standard approval regardless of urgency.

## Order Cancellation and Amendment
- A purchase order may be cancelled or amended without additional approval within **24 hours** of creation, provided fulfillment has not yet begun.
- After 24 hours, cancellation requires the same approval level as the original order.

## Recordkeeping
- All purchase orders, approvals, and supplier communications must be retained for a minimum of **3 years** for audit purposes.
