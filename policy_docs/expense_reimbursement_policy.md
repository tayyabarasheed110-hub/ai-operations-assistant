# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines the rules for submitting, approving, and reimbursing business-related employee expenses.

## Eligible Expenses
Eligible categories include: business travel (flights, trains, mileage), accommodation, client meals and entertainment, office supplies purchased on behalf of the company, and approved professional development costs.

## Receipt Requirements
- A receipt is **mandatory for any single expense of $25 or more**.
- Expenses under $25 may be submitted without a receipt, up to a maximum of **$100 in unreceipted claims per calendar month** per employee.
- Credit card statements alone are not accepted as proof of purchase; an itemized receipt or invoice is required.

## Approval Thresholds
- Expense claims **up to $500** require approval from the employee's direct manager only.
- Expense claims **between $500 and $2,000** require approval from the direct manager and the relevant department head.
- Expense claims **exceeding $2,000** require additional approval from Finance before reimbursement is processed.
- Any single expense line item **exceeding $5,000** requires prior written approval before the expense is incurred, not just at reimbursement time.

## Submission Deadlines
- Expense claims must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the expense being incurred.
- Claims submitted after **60 calendar days** will not be reimbursed except by explicit Finance exception approval.

## Meals and Entertainment
- Client meal expenses are capped at **$75 per person** unless pre-approved by a department head for a specific event.
- Alcohol is reimbursable only when part of a client meal and does not exceed **30% of the total bill**.

## Travel
- Domestic flights must be booked in economy class; business class requires department head pre-approval for flights **over 6 hours**.
- Mileage for personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the standard company rate of **$0.60 per mile**, capped at **500 miles per single trip** without prior approval.

## Reimbursement Timeline
- Approved claims are reimbursed within **10 business days** of final approval.
