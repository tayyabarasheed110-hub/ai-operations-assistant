# Leave Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines entitlement, notice, and approval rules for all forms of employee leave.

## Annual Leave Entitlement
- Full-time employees accrue **20 working days** of annual leave per calendar year.
- Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rata basis according to contracted hours.
- Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of **5 days** into the following calendar year. Any balance beyond 5 days is forfeited unless carried forward with written manager approval.

## Notice Periods
- Leave requests of **1–2 working days** require at least **2 working days'** advance notice.
- Leave requests of **3–5 working days** require at least **7 working days'** advance notice.
- Leave requests exceeding **5 working days** require at least **14 working days'** advance notice and written manager approval.
- Emergency leave (bereavement, sudden illness of a dependent) is exempt from notice requirements but must be reported to a manager within **24 hours** of the absence starting.

## Sick Leave
- Employees are entitled to **10 paid sick days** per calendar year.
- A medical certificate is required for sick leave lasting **more than 3 consecutive working days**.
- Sick leave exceeding **10 days** in a calendar year requires review by HR and may be reclassified as unpaid or extended leave.

## Approval Process
- Leave requests are submitted to the employee's direct manager.
- Requests exceeding **10 working days** additionally require sign-off from a department head.
- A manager must respond to a leave request within **3 working days** of submission; requests not responded to within this window are automatically escalated to HR.

## Unpaid Leave
- Unpaid leave may be granted for periods exceeding accrued entitlement, subject to manager approval.
- Unpaid leave requests exceeding **30 calendar days** require HR and department head approval and may affect continuous service calculations.

## Public Holidays
- Employees are entitled to all officially declared public holidays in addition to annual leave entitlement.
- If a public holiday falls on a scheduled rest day, an alternate day off is granted within the following **30 calendar days**.
